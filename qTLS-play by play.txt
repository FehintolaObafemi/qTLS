Initialization: Alice and Bob generate public and private keys using a quantum-safe key exchange algorithm such as the New Hope or Frodo KEM. Alice also generates a random session key 
for symmetric encryption.

Handshake: Alice and Bob exchange messages to agree on the cryptographic parameters to be used for the session, including the chosen quantum-safe cipher suite, the public key of the 
other party, and a random nonce.

Key exchange: Alice and Bob use their private keys to decrypt the other party's public key, and then use a quantum-safe key agreement protocol such as the NTRU or SIDH to generate a 
shared secret key for symmetric encryption.

Symmetric encryption: Alice and Bob use the shared secret key to encrypt and decrypt their messages using a quantum-safe symmetric cipher such as AES-GCM-SIV or ChaCha20-Poly1305.

Integrity protection: Alice and Bob use a quantum-safe message authentication code (MAC) such as Poly1305 or HMAC-SHA3 to ensure that their messages have not been tampered with.

Handshake completion: Once the handshake is complete and the session key has been securely exchanged, Alice and Bob can begin exchanging data using the symmetric encryption and integrity 
protection mechanisms.
